Child Safety Standards Policy

Last Updated: September 20, 2026

Akhil Bhartiya Kushwaha Mahasabha (ABKM) is committed to maintaining a safe, respectful, and secure environment for all community members. We have a zero-tolerance policy regarding any content, behavior, or activity that exploits or endangers children.

1. Zero Tolerance for Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) & Exploitation

ABKM strictly prohibits:

  • Any form of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE).
  • Grooming, harassment, or inappropriate communication directed toward minors.
  • Sharing or distribution of media involving the exploitation or abuse of minors.

Any account found violating these standards will be immediately and permanently banned, and reported to appropriate law enforcement authorities.

2. Content Moderation and Safety Controls

To uphold these child safety standards, ABKM employs proactive and reactive measures:

  • In-App Reporting: Users can report profiles, posts, or messages directly within the application.
  • Automated & Manual Review: Reported content is subjected to strict review by our safety and moderation team.
  • Account Suspensions: Accounts engaging in suspicious or harmful activity are immediately suspended pending investigation.

3. Reporting Child Safety Concerns

If you discover any content or interaction on ABKM that poses a risk to child safety, please report it immediately.

Dedicated Safety Contact Information

Email: support@futurelab.co.in

Response Time: Urgent child safety reports are reviewed and acted upon within 24 hours.

4. Law Enforcement & Regulatory Compliance

ABKM complies with all applicable local and international child protection laws. When CSAM or child exploitation is detected, we act swiftly to remove the content and report the incident to relevant authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and local law enforcement agencies (such as the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal in India).

5. Changes to This Policy

We regularly review and update our Child Safety Standards to comply with evolving regulations and Google Play Developer Policies.
